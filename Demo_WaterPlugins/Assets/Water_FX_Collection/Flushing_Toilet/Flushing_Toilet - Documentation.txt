A toilet with realistic flushing effects.

Press the left mouse button to flush the toilet.

Load "Flushing_Toilet.unity" for an example scene, or simply drag the Flushing_Toilet.prefab from the Prefabs folder (in the Project window) into the Hierarchy window.
